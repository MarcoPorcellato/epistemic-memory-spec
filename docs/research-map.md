# Research Map

## Authority order

Authority follows this order:

1. [STATUS.md](../STATUS.md), [CHARTER.md](../CHARTER.md), and published
   versioned decisions.
2. [VERSIONING.md](../VERSIONING.md) and other maintained governance and
   evidence documents.
3. [README.md](../README.md), [START_HERE.md](../START_HERE.md), and other
   navigation documents.

Navigation documents explain where to look; they do not override authoritative
status, governance, or versioned decisions.
