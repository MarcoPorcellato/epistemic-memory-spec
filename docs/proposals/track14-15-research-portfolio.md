# Proposal: clarify Track 14 and Track 15 portfolio relationship

**Proposed for maintainer discussion; not adopted.** This document proposes a
portfolio clarification. It does not change the epistemic core or authorise
tasks, materials, model access, evaluation, implementation, or extraction.

## Context

[Decision 0004](../decisions/0004-incubating-strategic-agent-learning.md)
incubates Explicit Strategic Learning as Track 14. [Decision 0005](../decisions/0005-incubating-multimodal-cognitive-scaffolding.md)
incubates Multimodal Cognitive Scaffolding as a separate, active Track 15.
Both are non-normative research tracks. Their stated questions overlap in
places, but neither decision establishes that their constructs are equivalent
or that either has demonstrated a benefit.

## Proposed disposition

Keep Track 14 and Track 15 as separately named, active research tracks. Add
cross-references between them where a future study compares strategic
transformation with modality-specific representation. Preserve separate
questions, falsifiers, controls, and governance gates until evidence or a
maintainer decision supports a different portfolio structure.

This disposition keeps Track 15 visible and available for dedicated research.
It does not treat a terminology comparison or a candidate's overlap with Track
14 as proof that the broader Track 15 question has collapsed.

## Review conditions and falsifiers

A later portfolio proposal may recommend containment, renaming, or retirement
only if it identifies which distinct prediction or governance need remains,
which is redundant, and what evidence supports that distinction. If an
outcome-free review shows that every Track 15 question can be expressed and
governed under Track 14 without loss, separate organization may be unnecessary.
If any Track 15 question needs distinct fidelity, exposure, renderer, or
capability controls, full containment would be misleading.

## Boundary

Any later proposal must preserve the distinction between evidence and
policy-derived assessment. Trivium, Quadrivium, Suggestopedia, TRIZ, and OTSM
remain comparison lenses or hypothesis sources, not architecture or proof.
“Accelerated” requires declared metrics and matched baselines. No safety,
alignment, transfer, novelty, or performance claim follows without direct
evidence for that claim.
