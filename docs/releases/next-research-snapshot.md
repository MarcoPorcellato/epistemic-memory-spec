## Release purpose and decision

Publish a citable account of the research completed since the first snapshot,
with accurate reading routes for people and agents. The proposed release
contains documentary research and corrections to discovery and citation
metadata. Its evidence supports review of the concepts and the next bounded
implementation decision.

Three approaches were considered. Releasing the merged documentary work gives
readers access to the existing prior-art reviews, strategic-learning boundaries,
Track 15 incubation and replay programme. Waiting for a qualified replay
implementation would tie these materials to unresolved execution work.
Publishing the local laboratory wholesale would require separate artifact,
privacy and reproducibility admission. The first approach is recommended,
with ORMA as an optional addition after its separately authorised merge.
Astra Medium assessed this boundary as PASS_WITH_NOTES on 2026-11-14.

The next practical development step is to qualify the execution method for
the existing local replay contract, then implement and check that bounded
operation under separate authority. Reuse the accepted local work and its
recorded failures. Keep the first prototype focused on inspectable replay
outcomes and their reasons.

observations and policy-derived assessments distinct, and consult the

snapshot during preparation. Selecting the next tag, release date and final
commit is a publication decision. At that point, citation metadata, README
links and release notes must be updated together.

## Route to the first useful prototype

Reuse the local minimal-utility plan, accepted G1–G4 bindings and G5 preflight
instead of restarting their design. Keep Q3 manual utility and later agent
comparisons outside the correctness work.

| Step | Required evidence | Stop or revision condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualify the execution proposal | Exact dependency/platform closure, interpreters and loading paths, enforceable resource/network/process/write boundaries, output retention and one-attempt rules. | An unverified control, unsupported platform, unresolved loader or missing receipt keeps execution blocked. |
| Authorise and implement the existing operation | Bind the exact accepted contracts and admitted synthetic inputs, future file map, offline dependency loading, review boundary and correction limits. | Contract incompatibility returns to its owner decision. Preserve accepted artifacts. |
| Check correctness under a separately authorised run | Compare exact outputs with independently reviewed static expectations, retain failure order and all terminal outcomes. | Invalid history, unavailable assessment and unavailable source reconstruction retain their distinct results. Shared agreement alone cannot rule out a shared mistake. |
| Evaluate usefulness only after correctness and a separate decision | Matched evidence and policy information, declared burden and decision criteria, retained negative or inconclusive outcomes. | Correctness alone leaves utility unanswered. |
| Consider later adapters and independent consumers | Exact consumer contract and separately produced evidence under G6/G7. | Integration remains conditional on the relevant repository and owner gates. |

The first prototype should make one operation reviewable from its admitted
inputs through its canonical result and permitted explanation. It should make
missing evidence, unsupported semantics and reconstruction limits inspectable.
The source remains independently available through its existing access path.
This proposed behaviour comes from the replay programme and G4 contract
boundary. It requires execution evidence before any performance claim.
