# Decision 0005: Incubating multimodal cognitive scaffolding without expanding the epistemic core

## Status

**Accepted for bounded incubation — maintainer decision (2026-09-05).**

This is a research-portfolio decision, not scientific acceptance of a
construct or result. It adds no schema, runtime, model update, task suite,
benchmark, executed protocol, interoperability claim, Matryca Plumber change,
or claim that any representation improves learning or performance.

## Context

The multimodal cognitive scaffolding pre-admission packet asks whether an
explicit transformation of frozen task information into a visual, spatial,
auditory, temporal, or affective representation can make a separately testable
contribution under semantic controls and matched exposure budgets.

The [admission-review memorandum](../../research/multimodal-cognitive-scaffolding-admission-review.md)
recorded a DEFER disposition because none of the six admission predicates was
proved at admission strength. That evidence remains unchanged. The maintainer
has nevertheless decided that the bounded question and its collapse conditions
merit an explicit research container. This is a portfolio choice about which
questions to preserve, not evidence that the construct exists, is distinct, or
is useful.

## Decision

Incubate **Multimodal Cognitive Scaffolding** as a separate, non-normative
**Track 15**. Keep the epistemic core unchanged.

For this track, a **candidate multimodal scaffold** is a provisional research
treatment: an explicitly specified transformation of frozen task information
into a representation whose incremental contribution could be tested against
matched controls. It is not evidence, an assessment, a source of authority, a
schema entity, a runtime abstraction, a selection policy, a candidate
strategic operator by default, or a claim about a model's internal state.

Track 15 provides a stable place to review terminology, prior art, controls,
falsifiers, and later protocols. It does not authorise an experiment or imply
that the track will survive its first dedicated review.

## Relationship to the historical DEFER disposition

This decision supersedes the 2026-09-05 DEFER memorandum **only as the current
research-portfolio disposition**. The direction is now accepted for bounded
incubation.

It does not supersede, negate, or satisfy the memorandum's source facts,
unknowns, falsifiers, no-claim boundaries, or six admission predicates. The
memorandum remains the historical evidence base explaining why incubation
begins fail-closed. In particular, this decision does not establish:

1. a distinct construct beyond prompting, retrieval, tools, procedures,
   representation learning, model capability, or candidate strategic
   operators;
2. a frozen bounded question, treatment, comparator set, or primary estimand;
3. executable semantic, relational, source, numerical, contradiction, or
   temporal fidelity audits;
4. a frozen learning regime, exposure boundary, model state, budget, or access
   policy;
5. an architectural reason for a separate implementation or repository; or
6. independent review of a future protocol or empirical result.

## Relationship to Track 14

Track 14 studies provisional reusable transformations of problem
representations or problem-solving processes. Track 15 studies whether a
modality-specific representation treatment remains a separately useful object
of inquiry after its information, generation procedure, presentation,
exposure, model capability, and selection policy are controlled.

A later protocol may test whether a particular scaffold implements,
approximates, or confounds a candidate strategic operator. It must not assume
that the terms are equivalent. If dedicated review finds no distinct
preregisterable prediction or governance need, Track 15 should be contained
within Track 14, renamed, or retired through a later decision.

## Evidence and assessment boundary

Any future evaluation must preserve the repository's existing distinction:

```text
canonical task information
representation treatment
observed outcome
provenance-bound evidence
named policy-derived assessment
```

An observed outcome is evidence only for its declared task, treatment,
comparator, model, environment, budget, and measurement. A later policy may
derive a bounded assessment from that evidence. Evidence is not assessment,
and assessment is not truth.

A scaffold asset is derived presentation material, not a canonical source and
not evidence that its content is correct. Canonical sources remain
authoritative.

## Falsification and collapse conditions

Track 15 must retain negative, failed, null, and non-interpretable outcomes.
The following conditions block promotion and may collapse a proposed
distinction:

- If an existing term such as prompt, retrieval, tool invocation, procedure,
  renderer effect, model capability, or candidate strategic operator fully
  describes the treatment and no incremental prediction remains, use the
  existing term.
- If treatment arms cannot preserve and audit the same canonical information,
  source relations, quantities, contradictions, and temporal content, the
  comparison is non-interpretable.
- If exposure, capability, model state, tools, retrieval, examples, feedback,
  compute, time, tokens, or human effort are unmatched or undeclared, no
  scaffold, learning, transfer, retention, or acceleration inference follows.
- If a result depends on post-hoc task, metric, treatment, or stopping choices,
  it cannot promote the construct.
- If the research question fits Track 14 without a separate governance need,
  a later decision should contain it there rather than preserve artificial
  separation.

## Claim boundaries

Same-turn conditioning, context retention, external-memory support,
non-parametric persistent state, and parametric learning are separate claims.
Cross-modal alignment, decodability, causal use, workspace participation,
retention, and learning are also separate claims.

“Accelerated” has meaning only for a preregistered metric and unit, a matched
baseline and budget, an uncertainty treatment, and disclosed regressions. No
claim of safety, alignment, transfer, novelty, retention, workspace use,
learning, acceleration, or performance follows without direct evidence that
matches that exact claim.

Trivium, Quadrivium, Suggestopedia, TRIZ, OTSM, mnemonic traditions, and other
human-learning frameworks remain comparison lenses or hypothesis sources only.
They are not architecture, proof, or evidence that a human result transfers to
an artificial agent.

## Research sequence and gates

The intended gated sequence is:

1. dedicated terminology, prior-art, and construct-separability review;
2. outcome-free preregistration of one bounded question, controls, budgets,
   failure states, and analysis;
3. separately authorised synthetic materials;
4. bounded evaluation retaining null, negative, failed, and
   non-interpretable outcomes;
5. independent review or replication; and
6. a continuation, containment, retirement, or eventual extraction decision.

Every gate requires separate maintainer authorisation. This decision creates
the track and its boundaries only. It does not authorise Phase 0 execution,
source acquisition, task generation, model access, evaluation, extraction, or
publication beyond this documentation change.

## Consequences

The multimodal direction is no longer deferred as a research-portfolio item;
it is visible as Track 15 and may receive a separately authorised Phase 0.
Scientific status does not change: the construct, separability, efficacy, and
governance need remain unproved.

The epistemic core remains unchanged. No runtime, schema, benchmark, model,
repository, or Matryca Plumber action follows from this decision.
