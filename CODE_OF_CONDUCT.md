# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this research repository a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of personal characteristics or background.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- showing empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive criticism; and
- focusing on what is best for the research record.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, intimidation, discrimination, sexualized language or attention, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private threats, and publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may take fair, proportionate corrective action. Reports should be made to the maintainer through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not publish an email address or sensitive evidence in a public issue.

## Scope and enforcement

These standards apply in repository spaces and wherever an individual is representing this repository. The maintainer will review reports, protect the reporter's privacy where practical, and communicate an outcome when possible.

This policy is based on Contributor Covenant 2.1.
