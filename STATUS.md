# Status

## Current state

**Research Draft — pre-standard.**

This repository is a dated public record of an investigation. It contains no
released protocol, stable schema, conformance suite, reference implementation,
or governance process for a standard.

## What this draft establishes

- a vocabulary for separating canonical assertions, observations, claims,
  evidence, assessments, procedural status, provenance, valid time, and
  recorded time;
- design constraints for a derived, privacy-preserving, human-governed claim
  projection; and
- conditions that must be met before any external interoperability claim is
  considered.

## What this draft does not establish

- factual truth, source reliability, authority, or user intent;
- a probability or confidence-calibration model;
- a canonical write path, autonomous correction system, or agent permission;
- compatibility with any product, database, agent framework, or organisation;
- a patent, copyright, trademark, priority, exclusivity, or legal ownership
  claim; or
- a promise that the research will become a standard.

## Advancement gates

A future revision may become a candidate technical specification only after it
defines all of the following:

1. closed schemas and canonical serialization;
2. deterministic replay and failure semantics;
3. privacy and retention boundaries;
4. explicit governance and change-control rules;
5. synthetic, provenance-recorded fixtures and a public conformance suite;
6. evidence from at least one implementation; and
7. a separate review before any claim of independent interoperability.

Until then, implementations should describe this work as inspiration or
research, not as a supported external standard.
