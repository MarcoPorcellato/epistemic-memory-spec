# Versioning

This repository uses three bounded maturity terms:

- **draft** — exploratory research that records concepts, constraints, and
  open questions; it is not a compatibility or conformance promise.
- **research snapshot** — a dated, immutable publication of the repository's
  research state; it remains non-normative and pre-standard.
- **candidate specification** — a proposed technical specification with
  explicit schemas, fixtures, review criteria, and implementation evidence.

Only a candidate specification may enter a conformance discussion, and only
after published schemas, fixtures, review criteria, and implementation
evidence. A research snapshot or draft cannot be presented as a standard,
protocol, production release, or interoperability claim.
