# Contributing

This repository is a public research draft, not a standard. Before opening an issue or pull request:

1. Identify the relevant document and section in [STATUS.md](STATUS.md), the [research draft](spec/epistemic-memory-research-draft-v0.md), or another maintained status or governance document.
2. Provide sources, exact references, or a reproducible counterexample. Distinguish observed evidence from interpretation.
3. Remove private vault content, prompts, credentials, absolute paths, and other sensitive material. Use synthetic or redacted examples.
4. Open an issue before proposing a pull request, so scope and evidence can be discussed publicly.

Pull requests should explain the research question addressed, cite affected sections, and include the checks needed to reproduce any claim. Keep changes narrow and maintainable. Do not present a draft contribution as a standard, compatibility guarantee, or production feature.

Accepted contributions remain non-normative until the versioning gates in [VERSIONING.md](VERSIONING.md) are met. Maintainer acceptance records research value; it does not create a requirement or conformance obligation.

## Maintainer review

The maintainer may request stronger sources, narrower claims, synthetic fixtures, or additional independent review. Issues and pull requests are public records; do not disclose material that requires private handling.
