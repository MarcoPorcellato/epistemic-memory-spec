# Issue-source — Track 14 Phase 0: review terminology and prior art for Explicit Strategic Learning

**Historical status:** Published as [issue #13](https://github.com/MarcoPorcellato/epistemic-memory-spec/issues/13), now closed. This document preserves the issue-source purpose and acceptance criteria; it is not a new publication request.

## Purpose

Complete the non-normative Phase 0 review required by Decision 0004 before any
experimental protocol is proposed. The work asks whether `candidate strategic
operator` is a useful, falsifiable research label after comparison with
established adjacent terminology.

## Scope

- Review continual and lifelong learning; transfer and generalisation;
  meta-learning; planning and tool use; active learning; programme synthesis;
  agent evaluation; and prompts, retrieval, traces, latent features, and model
  updates where needed for terminology.
- Treat TRIZ and OTSM only as possible sources of representation or
  contradiction hypotheses.
- Produce a cited terminology matrix for candidate strategic operator,
  heuristic, skill, procedure, policy, prompt, retrieved example, tool
  invocation, reasoning trace, latent feature or direction, and model update.
- State a falsification or collapse condition for every proposed distinction.
- Keep experimental evidence separate from any policy-derived assessment.

## Non-goals

- No schema, runtime, implementation, benchmark, model-training programme,
  external standard, compatibility claim, or Matryca Plumber change.
- No claim of safety, alignment, transfer, novelty, or performance.
- No claim that “accelerated” means more than a future preregistered metric
  against a matched baseline at a declared budget.
- No treatment of Trivium, Quadrivium, Suggestopedia, TRIZ, or OTSM as
  architecture or proof.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Review uses authoritative primary sources where available and labels
      source fact, synthesis, hypothesis, and unknown.
- [ ] Each terminology row states operational distinction, closest prior art,
      evidence needed, and falsification/collapse condition.
- [ ] Prior-art map covers all required research families.
- [ ] Review identifies source-exposure, retrieval, prompt, tool, trace, latent
      proxy, and parametric-update confounds where relevant.
- [ ] Any future acceleration claim names primary metric, units, matched
      baseline, budget, and relevant regressions.
- [ ] Maintainer review confirms no normative, vendor-specific, Plumber, or
      unsupported performance language.

## Exit condition

Phase 0 is complete only when maintainer review finds terminology bounded
enough to support a separate Phase 1 preregistration proposal, or records that
the construct collapses into existing terminology. Completion does not
authorise Phase 1, publication, implementation, or a new repository.
