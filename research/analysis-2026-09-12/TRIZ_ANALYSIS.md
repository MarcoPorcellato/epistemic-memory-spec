## System frame

**Useful function:** enable a maintainer and independent reader to determine what an epistemic-memory claim means, which evidence supports it, which assessment policy interprets it, and what must be checked next without overruling source authority.

**Observed insufficiencies:** research concepts are not closed executable contracts; experiment parameters and construct distinctions remain unresolved; the corpus has multiple navigation/status summaries and an invalid CFF type value. [Inventory](REPOSITORY_INVENTORY.md), [scientific review](SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW.md) and [source verification](SOURCE_VERIFICATION.md) separate these observations from their consequences.

**Potential harms, not measured outcomes:** premature claim promotion, duplicated maintenance, fragile citation parsing, ambiguous phase transitions, and spending on comparisons that cannot distinguish the proposed construct from a prompt or procedure.

**System boundary:** the research repository, its review and contribution process, and its optional future empirical programme. External products, private data, model training, source mutation and standards governance are outside this analysis's implementation authority.

**Invariants:** preserve [source authority and scope](../../CHARTER.md); keep evidence, policy-derived assessment and truth distinct; preserve the epistemic core under Decisions [0004](../../docs/decisions/0004-incubating-strategic-agent-learning.md) and [0005](../../docs/decisions/0005-incubating-multimodal-cognitive-scaffolding.md); retain null, negative and non-interpretable results; require matched metrics and budgets before acceleration claims.

**Ideal final result (IFR):** a reader obtains the correct bounded research answer and its next gate directly from maintained evidence, without an additional layer of duplicated status bureaucracy or unsupported authority. “Without existing” is used as a trimming thought experiment about that extra layer, not as a recommendation to erase the repository or its history.

## Contradictions and separation

Engineering contradictions:

- **E1:** IF all fifteen directions advance together, THEN more interesting hypotheses stay visible, BUT shared vocabulary and evidence boundaries can diverge and review capacity can fragment.
- **E2:** IF every overview repeats the current gate, THEN local discoverability improves, BUT each change creates more opportunities for contradictory status prose.
- **E3:** IF representations and strategic instructions become richer, THEN a task may become easier, BUT extra information, exposure or procedure can explain the apparent gain.
- **E4:** IF revision history remains fully reproducible, THEN past assessments can be inspected, BUT retained payloads or metadata can conflict with privacy and deletion requirements.
- **E5:** IF every enhancement gains a formal process artifact, THEN its constraints become explicit, BUT administrative cost can exceed the decision value.

E1–E5 describe plausible tensions. The audit has not measured their effect sizes. E3 and E4 are reasons to test and constrain future work, not findings of an existing experimental or privacy failure.

**Physical-contradiction analogue:** one overview must be short for orientation and detailed for independent evaluation. This is an information-design analogy, not a physical law.

| Separation | Applicability | Bounded resolution and failure condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time | Applicable | Read a short gate first, inspect details before decision. Fails if a reader can act on the short view without seeing required constraints. |
| Space | Applicable as document organization | Put details in linked evidence documents. Fails if links are broken, permissions prevent review, or the overview omits a governing boundary. |
| Condition | Applicable | Researcher, reviewer and maintainer paths expose relevant details while sharing the same facts. Fails if role-specific pages contradict one another. |
| Parts/whole | Applicable | Each evidence unit retains sufficient detail; the overview composes links rather than repeating claims. Fails if decomposition destroys a cross-track dependency or counterexample. |

For E4, deletion and replay cannot be promised simultaneously by this analogy. A separate threat-model and retention decision must specify what remains reconstructible and when honest rejection is required. No automatic erasure or eternal retention policy follows.

## Trimming without deleting evidence

| Tool | Action | Object | Result | Function |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Overview prose | Repeats status | Research track | Reader sees a gate locally | Orientation |
| Decision record | Specifies disposition and authority | Research track | Scope of acceptance is inspectable | Governance |
| Historical review | Preserves dated findings and unknowns | Proposed construct | Later decisions can be challenged | Evidence preservation |
| Issue form | Collects evidence and context | Contribution | Maintainer can route review | Intake |
| Review packet | Connects source, alternative and falsifier | Proposed next step | A decision can reject the proposal | Scientific discrimination |

**Candidate carrier to trim:** independently maintained repetitions of current status, not the historical reviews or their negative findings.

**Key problem:** IF duplicate status paragraphs are trimmed, THEN updates become less scattered, BUT readers may lose orientation. How can update burden fall without losing the governing boundary at the point of reading?

- **Rule A, remove the object:** inapplicable to scientific history and privacy warnings; their useful function remains. Applicable only if an actually redundant presentation has no distinct audience or decision function, as demonstrated by a reader check.
- **Rule B, object performs the function:** a decision or packet can state its own disposition, evidence date and next gate. This is a document-design analogy; the file does not autonomously validate itself.
- **Rule C, another component takes over:** existing decisions carry authority; a short link in each overview carries orientation. Preserve the link text “accepted for incubation; execution requires a separate gate” where needed. Do not create another equally authoritative status ledger.

No component has been removed. If the proposed route makes users miss a stop condition, keep the contextual repetition: minimal text is not the objective; reliable bounded decisions are.
