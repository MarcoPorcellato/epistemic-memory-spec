## Question and maintainer-reviewed disposition

The question reviewed is whether an explicitly specified transformation of the
same frozen task information into a visual, spatial, auditory, temporal, or
affective representation can make a separately testable contribution to a
preregistered task metric under semantic controls and matched exposure budgets.
It is a candidate question, not a claim that a modality, mnemonic, affective
treatment, or workspace mechanism helps.

**NO-GO — new numbered track now.** No separately testable construct or
architectural/governance separation has been demonstrated.

**NO-GO — containment in Track 14 now.** A future representation transform may
be evaluated as a possible candidate strategic operator, but no specific
transform, matched comparator set, or reviewed protocol yet shows that such a
containment decision is warranted. Track 14's current CSP Phase 1 proposal is
not this protocol.

Deferral preserves the candidate question without treating documentation
completion, a source map, or a review as evidence of efficacy, learning,
transfer, acceleration, safety, alignment, novelty, or performance.

## Evidence taxonomy

### Source facts

On 2026-09-05, the direct primary-source pages below were rechecked for their
publication identity and bounded scope. The reported findings remain limited to
each source's stated model, method, task, and measurement.

- [Hsu et al. (2023)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v239/hsu23a.html) is a PMLR
  NeurIPS-workshop proceeding. Its reported Visual-Scratchpad method combines
  diagram execution, visual readout, and optionally fine-tuned expert
  iteration; it does not isolate an external representation treatment.
- [Hou et al. (2025)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/hou25c.html) is an
  ICML/PMLR proceeding. Its evaluated LVLMs had limited relational diagram
  understanding and showed background-knowledge shortcuts in that tested suite.
- [Lei, Xiao, and Wei (2026), arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02673v1)
  reports a teacher-trace, graph-mind-map guidance pipeline. It is a preprint
  whose reported comparison bundles teacher traces, rendering, fine-tuning, and
  distillation; it is not a general scaffold or learning result.
- [Gurnee et al. (2026)](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html)
  is a research article about workspace-like, verbalizable representations in
  studied language models. It is model-specific and does not establish that an
  external image or audio scaffold enters a workspace.
- A direct availability request to the [ImageBind CVF page](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Girdhar_ImageBind_One_Embedding_Space_To_Bind_Them_All_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
  returned HTTP 403 during this review, so its page content was not live
  reverified here. This is an availability limitation, not a negative scientific
  finding.

### Synthesis

The reviewed literature supports a tightly bounded research question about
derived representations and confounds. It does not establish a construct that
is independent from prompting, retrieval, tool use, procedures, representation
learning, or Decision 0004's candidate strategic operator. The latter remains
a provisional reusable transformation of a problem representation or process;
it is not a schema field, runtime abstraction, internal representation, prompt,
tool call, trace, or latent direction.

### Hypotheses

- A frozen representation treatment may make an incremental contribution to one
  bounded metric after semantic and exposure parity controls.
- A representation, its generation procedure, a treatment, a selection policy,
  and a candidate strategic operator may make separable predictions in one
  later protocol.
- A memorable treatment may improve one outcome while worsening source,
  relational, contradiction, or temporal fidelity.

These are hypotheses only. They do not authorise task generation, a
preregistration execution, model access, or a performance claim.

### Unknowns

- Whether a representation treatment survives matched content, exposure,
  renderer, and model-capability controls.
- Whether a valid task family can measure all relevant fidelity dimensions
  without adding answer hints.
- Whether an apparent effect is instead a prompt, retrieval, tool, procedure,
  model-capability, or example effect.
- Whether any bounded representation-transform question needs governance beyond
  a narrower Track 14 study.
- Whether a legitimate held-out transfer, retention, causal-use, or workspace
  claim can be supported at all.

## Why defer, rather than the alternatives

**Rather than admit:** predicates 1–6 are unproved. In particular, no separate
architectural reason or frozen, outcome-free protocol exists.

**Rather than contain now:** containment would still require an explicit
maintainer decision about a named transform and a reviewed comparator design.
Treating the general topic as already contained would blur a scaffold,
procedure, treatment, selection policy, and operator before their separability
is tested.

**Rather than reject:** the review defines a falsifiable candidate question and
specific collapse conditions. Absence of current evidence is not evidence that
no bounded question can be formulated.

**Counterargument:** source coverage, confound controls, completed document
reviews, and a post-merge checkpoint make the direction well prepared.
**Response:** they establish review quality and a possible future test, not a
separate construct, outcome, causal mechanism, or governance need.

## Minimum falsifier and missing evidence

The minimum falsifier for a separate-track case is an independent
construct-separability audit of one proposed transformation showing that it is
fully captured by a prompt, procedure, retrieval, tool, or Track 14 candidate
operator treatment and makes no incremental preregisterable prediction under
matched semantic and exposure controls. The separate-track case then collapses;
it does not become evidence for a weaker claim.

Admission-strength evidence still missing is: one named transform; a frozen
task family and source-exposure boundary; matched prompt, retrieval, tool, and
procedure controls; a primary estimand and uncertainty method; bounded token,
time, compute, tool, retrieval, feedback, and human-effort budgets; outcome-free
fidelity audits; negative and non-interpretable outcome handling; independent
protocol review; and a concrete reason why Track 14 governance is insufficient.

“Accelerated” remains meaningful only for a preregistered metric with a matched
baseline and declared budget, plus disclosed regressions. Evidence must remain
distinct from any later policy-derived assessment, and assessment remains
distinct from truth.

## Risks and retained boundaries

Human-learning traditions, Trivium, Quadrivium, Suggestopedia, TRIZ, and OTSM
remain comparison lenses or hypothesis sources only. They are not architecture,
proof, or evidence that a human result transfers to an agent.

This memorandum makes no claim about model consciousness, felt emotion, safety,
alignment, retention, transfer, novelty, workspace participation, causal use,
or performance. It introduces no epistemic-core change and authorises no
schema, runtime, policy, model update, implementation, benchmark, task suite,
Plumber change, or new repository.
