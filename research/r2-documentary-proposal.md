## Scope

The proposed documents describe provisional research choices and falsifiers.
They create no normative protocol, schema, fixture, implementation, model
experiment, conformance claim, or change to the published decisions.

## Review limits

The source comparison cites public primary technical material and makes no
claim that the selected sources are complete or that their profiles are
adopted. Model-assisted editorial checks, if reported during preparation, are
not independent scientific review, replication, implementation, or empirical
evidence.

## Completion states

- Local document preparation means exact candidate bytes exist for review.
- Publication requires issue-first review, a closed link set, privacy/source
  claim checks, current base/head review, applicable repository checks, and
  separate maintainer authority.
- Publication would still not complete the research programme or any
  preregistration, synthetic task, evaluation, implementation, or independent
  interoperability gate.

“Accelerated” requires declared metrics and matched baselines. Evidence remains
distinct from policy-derived assessment. The candidate strategic operator
remains a research construct, not a schema or runtime entity.
